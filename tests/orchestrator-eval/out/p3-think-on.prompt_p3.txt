**FRAMEWORK SEMANTIC TRAP:**  
- `timestamp` in `Complaint` dataclass **must** be timezone-aware `datetime` (e.g., `datetime.now(timezone.utc)`). Naive datetime objects will cause display failures per "timezone-aware form" requirement.  
- Date display **must** use `complaint.timestamp.year`, `.month`, `.day` (not `strftime`). `strftime` with `%Y, %m, %d` would include timezone offsets, violating "year, month, day" format.
